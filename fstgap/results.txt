FST outer-sensor gap offset -- measured results
===============================================
Sample: 152 matched files of runs 23081008 / 23081015, run twice through
fwd_afterburner_db.C with StFwdHitLoader::setApplyFstGapFix off and on.
~58M unbiased-residual rows per side. Files lacking a readable alignTree
were excluded from BOTH samples.

This is the numbers only. The working notes, including the preliminary
pass and the analysis dead ends, are in gapfix_test/CONCLUSIONS.txt.

3. TRACK FIT QUALITY
--------------------
chi2/ndf of the leave-one-out refit, by which hit was removed:

  removed hit      OFF                ON              change
  OUTER        7.5243+-0.0080   7.2834+-0.0075   -0.241+-0.011  21.9 sig  3.2%
  inner        7.8594+-0.0034   7.8206+-0.0034   -0.039+-0.005   8.1 sig  0.49%

Population-controlled, d(chi2/ndf) = ON - OFF at fixed nPointsUsed:

  nPts    6      7      8      9     10     11     12     13     14
  OUTER -.414  -.393  -.298  -.283  -.301  -.075  -.096  +.043  -.111
  inner -.059  -.052  -.050  -.044  -.037  -.026  -.005  +.013  +.029
  ratio  7.0    7.6    6.0    6.4    8.1     --     --     --     --

The control is NOT zero (8.1 sig inclusive, 3-4 sig at low nPts): moving
outer hits changes the fits inner residuals are measured against. The
result rests on the 6-8x ratio, not on a null control. The effect dies by
nPts 11-12, where more fit points let the fit absorb one displaced hit.

6. WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
----------------------
- Does not change the 12-fold wedge structure: RMS of the 12 per-wedge
  means unchanged to <=0.002 cm.
- Does not make the absolute fit good: chi2/ndf ~7.8, nowhere near 1.
  FST pull widths are 0.161 (U) / 0.555 (V) with 73.8% of rows outside a
  1 cm core. The covariance model dominates the chi2.
- Does not apply to MC. StFstFastSimMaker never applies a gap term, so
  there is no displacement to remove and the correction would introduce
  one.
