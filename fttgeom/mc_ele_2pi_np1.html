Electron-gun MC: full 2π, 1 electron/event, 4000 events

← Back to FST-blind projection study | Other MC samples: single e- @ 45° | 2π, 8 e-/event

Setup

  • Single-electron gun per event, full azimuth (0<φ<2π), pt=1.5GeV, 2.5<η<4.0, vz=0, 4000 events (script/genElectron2pi.C(4000,1,1)).
  • Same real reconstruction chain and FST-blind diagnostic as the other MC pages -- StFttDbMaker → StFttSimHitMaker → StFttClusterMaker → StFttClusterPointMaker(mUseGeantData=false) → fwdTrack (script/simFttResidualTest.C).

Findings

  • Precise coordinate still shows a real peak at dx≈0, but now with visible shoulders out to ±5-7cm rather than the near-delta spike seen in the single-quadrant 45° sample -- expected, since events now span all strip lengths/pitches across the full azimuth instead of one fixed quadrant's strips.
  • Off-axis coordinate is a broad real hump (not flat, not a tight spike) -- a middle ground between the 45° sample's tight peak and real data's flat background. Makes sense: still only one track/event (little combinatoric background), but now mixing many different strip lengths/positions across 2π.
  • 2D maps: candidates form a genuine vertical (V) / horizontal (H) band around the true hit, wider than the 45° sample but still clearly correlated, not a flat landscape.
  • Still one track/event, so this sample alone still can't test the real-data "closest-candidate artifact" question -- see the 8 e-/event page for that.
