Electron-gun MC: full 2π, 8 electrons/event, 4000 events

← Back to FST-blind projection study | Other MC samples: single e- @ 45° | 2π, 1 e-/event

Setup

  • 8 electrons/event, full azimuth (0<φ<2π), pt=1.5GeV, 2.5<η<4.0, vz=0, 4000 events total -- split into 8 condor jobs of 500 events each (condor/submit_ele2pi_np8.txt, script/runElectronFttReal), merged with hadd.
  • Same real reconstruction chain and FST-blind diagnostic as the other MC pages -- StFttDbMaker → StFttSimHitMaker → StFttClusterMaker → StFttClusterPointMaker(mUseGeantData=false) → fwdTrack (script/simFttResidualTest.C).
  • Purpose: 8 tracks/event gives real combinatoric background/occupancy, closer to real data than the single-track samples -- built specifically to test whether real data's narrow peak is a genuine correlation or an artifact of always picking the closest candidate.

Findings

  • "All candidates" is now a genuine flat combinatoric background (~500-600 entries/bin), similar occupancy character to real data -- unlike the single-track samples, where "All" was itself already a tight cluster.
  • A real peak sits on top of that background, in the raw "All candidates" population itself, before any nearest-hit selection is applied. The precise coordinate (V:dx) rises from a ~500-600/bin flat background to ~13,000 at dx≈0 -- about 20x above background, visible directly in the unconditioned candidate population. A flat/uniform underlying distribution cannot produce that on its own; this is direct evidence the correlation is physical, not a selection artifact.
  • 2D maps make this explicit: a genuine flat background landscape (±60-80cm) with a separate, tight vertical/horizontal spike at the true hit location, clearly distinguishable by eye from the background.
  • Off-axis coordinate (V:dy, H:dx) is now a broad hump for both orientations, similar in character to real data's V behavior -- the sharp V-vs-H asymmetry seen in real data (H flat, V peaked) is not obviously reproduced here; both look more alike in this MC sample.
  • Relevant to the real-data page's conclusion: this sample directly tests the "narrow peak is an artifact of picking the closest candidate at ~20% true efficiency" hypothesis from the real-data page under comparable occupancy, and does not support it -- the peak here is real and present before selection. Real data may still differ (this is a single-species, single-pt gun, not real event topology/pileup), but the base mechanism proposed for real data is not reproduced under matched occupancy in MC.
