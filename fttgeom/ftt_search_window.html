FTT hit-matching purity: is the FTT hit a track picks up actually the right one?

Method

  • Every candidate FTT hit on a disk, and the one actually used by tracking, are compared against an FST+vertex-only track projection that has never seen any FTT hit -- a genuinely FTT-blind reference.
  • Split by strip orientation: V (vertical strip) measures x precisely, y only to the strip's own physical extent ("off-axis"); H (horizontal strip) is the mirror image.
  • Plots below: single-file, 500-event real-data test (all 4 disks), raw counts (no area normalization), off-axis range ±35cm.
  • The 4 matching/geometry fixes behind this study (maxStripLength, maxStripCenter, the off-axis search gate, the covariance unit fix) are listed on the FTT geometry index page.

Electron-gun MC

Same FST-blind diagnostic, on controlled MC samples with known truth, through the real reconstruction chain -- StFttDbMaker → StFttSimHitMaker → StFttClusterMaker → StFttClusterPointMaker(mUseGeantData=false) → fwdTrack -- not the GEANT-truth blur used elsewhere for MC. Three samples, increasing occupancy:

  • Single e- @ 45° (1000 events) -- one track/event, phi restricted to one quadrant.
  • 2π, 1 e-/event (4000 events) -- one track/event, full azimuthal range.
  • 2π, 8 e-/event (4000 events) -- 8 tracks/event, full azimuthal range, closer to real multi-track occupancy.

→ Real-data excess check -- the same question checked directly on real data with ~1000x the statistics of the single-file plots below: is there a real correlation in the raw candidate population, and what fraction of matched hits are genuinely correct? Current best numbers and plots live there, not below.
