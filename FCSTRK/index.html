Forward ECAL Cluster in Tracking (FCSTRK, TrkType=5)

  • Goal: Add FCS ECAL clusters to the Genfit forward track refit to improve momentum resolution for J/ψ→e+e- and Drell-Yan electrons. FTT covers z=281–349 cm; ECAL is at z=710 cm. The extra 361 cm lever arm improves the exit-angle (slope) measurement and thus momentum resolution.
  • Key question: What σpos to use in the Genfit PlanarMeasurement covariance for the ECAL cluster hit? Theory (Shashlyk Pb/scintillator, PHENIX PbSc): σpos ≈ 0.5/√E + 0.2 cm → 0.35 cm at E=15 GeV. But is this achieved in STAR FCS simulation?
  • Code: /star/u/akio/fcstrk11/star-sw-fwd (branch: dev)
  • Momentum resolution gain (Fisher information estimate)
    • 4 FTT planes at z=281,304,327,349 cm, σFTT=1 mm/hit, centroid at z=315 cm: Islope,FTT = 261,096 cm−2 ⇒ σ(slope)FTT = 1.96 mrad
    • Adding one ECAL cluster at z=710 cm (lever arm Δz=395 cm from FTT centroid): Islope,ECAL = (Δz)2/σpos2
      • σpos=0.35 cm (PHENIX PbSc formula, same 5.54 cm cells, E=15 GeV): IECAL=1,270,000 → ∼58% improvement in slope/momentum
      • σpos=1.2 cm (measured in STAR FCS MC, with centroid weighting, E=15 GeV): IECAL=108,000 → ∼19% improvement
      • σpos=1.6 cm (no centroid weighting, cell center only): IECAL=61,000 → ∼11%
      • σpos=3 cm (uncorrected truth, z-mismatch systematic): IECAL=17,200 → ∼3%
    • Centroid weighting is implemented in StFcsClusterMaker and working. Flat-E γ gun measures σpos≈0.9–1.4 cm (decreasing with E) after correcting for truth-z vs cluster-z mismatch (z=710 vs zshowermax≈725 cm). This is larger than the PHENIX formula but the PHENIX formula may not apply at this cell size; actual STAR FCS resolution needs validation with data.
  • What the cluster-track residual measures
    • σres2 = σpos2 + σtrk_proj2. Primary tracks (constrained to vz=0, trkType=2) are extrapolated by Genfit from z≈349 cm (last FTT plane) to z=710 cm (361 cm further), crossing the solenoid return-yoke fringe field — complex, non-uniform B-field.
    • The measured residual σres=2–5 cm is dominated by σtrk_proj. Evidence: a simple helix in uniform Bz=0.5 T gives 8.9 cm RMS (worse than Genfit Primary 4.6 cm RMS), confirming that the real field is too complex for analytic propagation.
    • The 2–5 cm is the matching window to find the right cluster; the Genfit covariance must use σpos≈0.35 cm.

  • FCSTRK Implementation (TrkType=5) — DONE (2026-06-27)
    • Start from BLCVtx track (trkType=4); project to zshowermax≈725 cm.
    • Find closest ECAL cluster within a dynamic matching window (2026-06-29): window = 4σ × √(σu2 + σv2 + σcluster2) where σu, σv are the position uncertainties on the FCS plane extracted from the Genfit propagated covariance matrix after projectToPlane() (state vector indices 3,4 in the (q/p, u′, v′, u, v) parametrization), and σcluster=1.2 cm. Clamped to [5, 50] cm. Low-pT tracks bent further in the fringe field get a wider window; high-pT tracks stay near 5–10 cm. No E/p cut (currently).
    • Add matched cluster as Genfit SpacepointMeasurement (kTpcId) with σpos=1.2 cm (measured in STAR FCS MC, see above).
    • Refit → mTrackType = kFCSTRKstrained = 5.
    • New enum: StFwdTrack::kFCSTRKstrained=5; predicate isFCSTRKstrainedTrack().
    • Implementation: doFCSTRKstrainedFitting() in FwdTracker.h; cluster loading block in StFwdTrackMaker.cxx; enum in StFwdTrack.h.
  • Results (2026-06-27, updated 2026-06-29, final) (sample: flat electron, E=1–40 GeV, η∈[2.5,4.0], φ=2π)
    • Bug fixes:
      • Looper track skip (FwdTracker.h): Low-pT tracks looping in the 0.5 T field caused projectToPlane() to z=725 cm to throw a genfit::Exception. Fixed with try/catch: looper tracks are now cleanly skipped (no FCSTRK track produced).
      • Shower secondary filter (picoMatch.C): MC electron loop included EM shower secondaries. Added |p|>1 GeV/c cut on the MC 3-momentum magnitude (mc->p().Mag()). Gun electrons have E≥1 GeV ⇒ |p|≈E≥1 GeV/c (ultra-relativistic); shower secondaries have |p|≪1 GeV/c.
    • FCSTRK conditional efficiency (BLCVtx → FCSTRK conversion): 5883 / 10033 BLCVtx tracks = 58.6% overall. Diagnostic counters from BFC log (aggregated over 10 runs) identify the ~41% loss:
      Loss categoryTracksFraction of BLCVtxRecoverable?
      True loopers (fail at z=725 and z=500 cm)114413.8%No — physics limit
      No matching cluster at z=725 cm102212.3%Partially (NS gap, η≈4 hole, low-E threshold)
      Refit failed after cluster match2483.0%Minor
      Fallback via z=500 cm + straight line3≈0%Negligible
      Conclusion: the efficiency plateau is a physics limit. True loopers (pT < 0.3–0.5 GeV/c, radius < ~3 m in 0.5 T) cannot reach z=725 cm and also fail at z=500 cm — the fallback straight-line approach yields essentially nothing. The “no cluster” loss (12.3%) is dominated by NS gap (φ≈±π/2), η≈4 inner hole, and low-E showers below the cluster threshold. No algorithmic improvement can recover the looper fraction. (A helix-based denominator was tried first but is unreliable — the complex fringe field between z=200 and z=710 cm makes simple helix propagation wrong.)
