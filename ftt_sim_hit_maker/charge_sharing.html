Real FTT charge-sharing measurement

← back to StFttSimHitMaker main page

Goal

  • Replace StFttSimHitMaker's flat 1:8:1 3-strip model with a real, measured profile.
  • Source: production p+p 500GeV MuDst, run 23081015, 2463 events.

Method

  • No existing FTT cluster QA tool found; StMuFttCluster has no row(), and the cluster↔rawhit link (StMuFttUtil::rebuildRelationships()) is an empty stub — so re-clustering from raw hits directly, in a new StRoot/StFttChargeSharingMaker.
  • Time cut: real per-VMM anchor learned online from each VMM's dbcid mode (same algorithm as StFttHitCalibMaker, 200-sample threshold), window [-65,100] (confirmed from DB, constant for every VMM this run).
  • Re-clustering: same algorithm as StFttClusterMaker (max-ADC anchor, expand while adjacent + non-increasing ADC). Note: real threshold is currently unset (GetThresholdFor() stub returns 0) — any nonzero ADC, including noise, can join a cluster.
  • 57.9% of raw hits pass the time cut; 5,634,704 clusters from 2443 analyzed events.

First peak in cluster total ADC (sumAdc≈50-100)

  • That peak is essentially all nStrips==1 clusters (split sumAdc by multiplicity: n=1 peaks at sumAdc≈90 and is nearly gone by 300; n≥2 peaks at sumAdc≈220 and dominates above that).
  • Per-channel raw ADC (before any clustering, straight from MuDst) has a hard floor: zero hits below ADC≈15, sharp rise, broad hump ≈60-90, then smooth decline. This is a real hardware/DAQ zero-suppression threshold already in the data — not from our clustering (whose own threshold is off/0).
  • So the low-sumAdc peak = real single-strip hits whose only one strip (and no neighbor) cleared that hardware threshold. Not an artifact of this analysis.
  • Real hit vs. noise: can't fully separate without a pedestal run, but the shape argues for real hits — the n==1 decline spans ~300 ADC counts, much broader than a narrow noise excess sitting just above threshold would give; looks more like the natural low-charge tail of real hits being clipped by the threshold.
  • Comb/moiré pattern in the ADC plots is a binning artifact (200 bins over 0-1024 vs. integer ADC values), not physical.

Caveat

  • Falloff vs. offset is much heavier-tailed than a clean Gaussian (roughly ×2 drop per strip out to ±4, tail out to 10 strips).
  • Zero threshold means noise and unrelated pileup (busy p+p event, wide time window) can inflate the tails — not separated from genuine single-hit charge spread.
  • Decided: full deconvolution (threshold + pileup jointly) is more than a tracking detector sim needs. Model scope limited to center strip ±1 (below).

Model implemented in StFttSimHitMaker

  • Gaussian(σ) charge response, split over center strip ±1 only (far tail dropped — small, 2.8% of clusters, likely pileup/noise contaminated here).
  • σ=2.4mm (pitch=3.2mm), fit to the clean nStrips==3 ratio 25:48:25.
  • Split uses the TRUE sub-strip hit position (previously discarded by rounding) — asymmetric per hit, not a fixed ratio; restores the real charge-division resolution benefit.
  • Total charge/hit: exponential draw, mean=431.5 (measured sumAdc mean=RMS), drawn ONCE per truth hit and shared between the intended + diagonal orientation (same foil, same physical charge deposit — see X-U/Y-V correlation check below).
  • ~70 ADC threshold applied once, after summing all contributions per channel, to neighbor strips only. Center strip is always kept (floored to threshold if the draw came in low) — a naive all-3-strips threshold silently dropped ~30% of truth hits entirely, an unintended efficiency loss (this project keeps efficiency at 100% for now, see TODO on main page).

  • Scanned both σ (1.6-5.8mm) and mean charge (300-2100 ADC) separately: neither knob can reach the real 1:2:3 shape (renormalized: 24%:41%:34%). 2-strip stays stuck around 25-27% regardless — a structural limit of a symmetric-Gaussian + hard-threshold model, not a tuning problem. Likely needs a smeared/noisy threshold or non-Gaussian response to fix properly — judged out of scope (same call as the deconvolution decision above).
  • Kept σ=2.4mm, mean=431.5 as-is (both anchored to real measurements). The two things that matter for tracking -- position-dependent resolution benefit, 100% baseline efficiency -- are in place; the multiplicity mix is approximate.
  • nStrips>3 can only come from nearby-track cluster merges in this model (one hit is capped at 3 strips by construction) — real data's 4+ tail includes genuine wide single-hit spread, so this is a structural, accepted difference, not a bug.

Same-foil charge correlation (X-U, Y-V)

Vertical(X)+DiagonalV(U) share the front foil; Horizontal(Y)+DiagonalH(V) share the back foil — same physical charge deposit, two differently-angled strip layers. Checked whether that shows up as a real correlation.

1st pass: sum of all hits in the event (too easy)

  • Total in-time ADC per plane/quad/event, one layer vs. the other — NOT per-cluster, just aggregate occupancy.
  • Both show a clear diagonal ridge: correlation coefficient 0.60 (X-U), 0.81 (Y-V).
  • Diagonal layer consistently collects less total charge: U/X mean ratio 0.59, V/Y mean ratio 0.60 — same ratio on both foils, looks systematic (diagonal-strip geometry/gain), not noise.
  • But: this correlates almost trivially just from occupancy (more hits ⇒ more charge on both layers together) — doesn't test whether individual CLUSTERS match.
X (Vertical) vs U (DiagonalV), event sumsY (Horizontal) vs V (DiagonalH), event sums

2nd pass: cluster-by-cluster, all combinations

  • Every (X-cluster,U-cluster) combination within the same plane/quad/event (same for Y/V) — true matches should sit on top of the combinatorial background from unrelated pairings.
  • No peak survives: correlation coefficient drops to 0.017 (X-U), 0.028 (Y-V) at full statistics (51M / 55M combinations). The ratio histograms (U/X, V/Y) are smooth and featureless, no bump near 0.6-0.8 — pure combinatorial background swamps any real signal.
  • Why: ~144 clusters per (disk,quad) per event combining all 4 orientations (~36 per single orientation) — a p+p event doesn't have that many real particles per quadrant. Cross-checked against PRODUCTION's own already-reconstructed StMuFttCluster count (not just this re-clustering) for the same 40 real events: production shows the SAME order of magnitude (~1000-2700 clusters/event). Not a bug in this analysis — StFttClusterMaker::GetThresholdFor() is a real, still-unimplemented stub (returns 0) in production too, so real reconstruction is currently just as noise/pileup-dominated as this re-clustering.
  • Tried requiring EXACTLY 1 X-cluster + 1 U-cluster (no combinatorial ambiguity at all) to isolate clean pairs: zero such plane/quad/events exist anywhere in the full 2463-event file. Occupancy is uniformly too high, everywhere, all the time, in this sample — there's no quiet corner to exploit.

Conclusion: a ratio cut can't identify correlated cluster pairs in this dataset as-is — the real fix is upstream (implement GetThresholdFor() to cut noise/pileup clusters before combinatorics gets tried at all), not a downstream ratio cut fighting an already-overwhelming background.
