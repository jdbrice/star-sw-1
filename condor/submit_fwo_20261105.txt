# FST OUTER-SENSOR OVERLAP, large sample.  Run24 zero-field, FieldOff.
#
# Purpose: PER-BOUNDARY overlap widths. The 120k-event single-process run measured
# an outer overlap of 5.2 strips against 4.27 nominal, and splitting it by r-strip
# gave a slope of -0.17 where pure pitch predicts 0.00 and a pure radial shift of
# d = -0.176 cm predicts -0.40 -- a mixture, pitch ~ +0.46% plus d ~ -0.7 mm
# (commit d8c8e1ccfb, and FstOuterSensorOverlap/index.html section 6).
#
# That decomposition assumes the widths are not partly faked by a per-wedge
# rotation pattern: coincidence YIELD is non-linear, so unlike the pairing centroid
# it does not cancel rotations. Only per-boundary widths separate the two --
# geometry is common to all 36 boundaries, a rotation pattern is not, and it is
# antisymmetric between a wedge's two ends where geometry is not.
#
# 48 jobs x 30 files (1433 files, ~12.9M events, ~100x the published sample).
# The job needs no DB and no fGeom.root -- the measurement reads MuDst FST strip
# indices only, which is what makes it independent of the conventions it tests.
#

#   hadd -f /gpfs01/star/pwg_tasks/FwdCalib/akio/fwo_20261105/fwo_merged.root /gpfs01/star/pwg_tasks/FwdCalib/akio/fwo_20261105/fwo_*.root
