Biased FST/FTT Residuals (StFwdResidualMaker)

Preliminary study, superseded. This is the original residual check, done before StFwdAlignmentMaker (the unbiased, hit-removed maker) existed. The residual here is pulled toward the hit by the fit that produced it, so misalignments are underestimated — treat this as a rough first look only. The current main analysis is on the residual index page.

  • Code: StRoot/StFwdResidualMaker (StFwdResidualMaker.h, .cxx)
  • Plot macro: script/plotFwdResidual.C
  • Goal: simple first-pass check for large misalignments, ahead of the full multi-parameter (xyz+3 angle per element) alignment fit
  • Method: per-plane residual between the hit used in the fit and the track's fitted projection at that z
    • Since the hit is in the fit, the residual is pulled toward the hit — misalignments are underestimated (rough first look, not the final alignment)
    • FST residual = r·Δφ = r·(φhit − φproj), folded to (−π,π)
    • FTT residual = Δx (V-strip) or Δy (H-strip), picked by hit covariance. Diagonal U/V strips not yet used in the fit.
  • Plane usage: per track type (Global/BLC/Primary/FwdVtx/BLCVtx/FCSConstrained), fraction of tracks with a hit/vertex/cluster on each plane
  • Data source: StEvent::fwdTrackCollection() by default, or StMuDst::muFwdTrackCollection() via setUseMuDst(true) for standalone MuDst analysis — see debug/index.html Issue #27
  • Residual axis range: FST ±1 cm, FTT ±2.5 cm
  • Outlier summary — auto-generated (script/summarizeFwdResidual.C): usage/offset table, plus translation/rotation/length-scale misalignment-mode tests. Covers both subpages below.
