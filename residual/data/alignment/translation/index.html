Conclusion

  • A single global sin(φ-φ0) is still not a good model even after correction — χ²/ndf drops by roughly two orders of magnitude vs. uncorrected (see distributions.html for the uncorrected 12-fold step pattern this correction targets), but stays at ~80-140, well above 1. Some real structure remains.
  • Root cause of the original step pattern (candidate, not confirmed): in official STAR software, StFstHitMaker::Make() computes a per-sensor DB/survey alignment correction via LocalToMaster() and then does not apply it. Open caveats (not yet checked): whether Survey_st is actually populated with validated offsets for FST, and whether the overwrite is intentional (a deliberate stopgap, per the code's own "need to revisit" comment) rather than a defect.
  • The per-wedge phi correction absorbs 42-63% of the step amplitude (see wedgecorrection/index.html) but is a single mean offset per (disk,wedge) from 150-file-averaged stats — it can't capture structure finer than one wedge, which may explain what's left (see the straight-line segments noted above, and the "why the steps aren't fully eliminated" discussion on the correction page).
