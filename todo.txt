2. sigma(q/pT) degradation at Ne/evt = 4, 8
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Related to #1 but framed as a resolution question. Candidate causes to
check:
  - Occupancy: more hits per event -> more ambiguous hit-to-track
    assignment during seeding.
  - Search windows too wide: with more hits present, a window sized for
    low-occupancy running may pick up the wrong hit more often.
  - Compare seeding efficiency / fake rate vs Ne/evt to isolate seeding
    from fitting.

3. More BLC tracks does not improve the BLC vertex -- why?
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Never figured this out. Naively more tracks -> better z_vtx precision
(smaller sigma_vtx from the weighted mean in doBLCVertexFitting()), but
that's not what's observed. Worth checking:
  - Is the weighting (w_i = nFitPoints) actually discriminating
    good/bad tracks, or are bad tracks getting similar weight to good
    ones and diluting the vertex?
  - Is the 3-sigma outlier rejection loop in doBLCVertexFitting()
    actually converging, or oscillating/stalling with more input tracks?
  - Could be the same high-occupancy effect as #1/#2 feeding in bad
    tracks that outnumber the good ones as Ne/evt grows.

6. Dilepton mixed-event mass-shape structure -- still unresolved
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
The real-data (202607) mixed-event-subtracted dilepton mass spectrum
has a persistent, genuine (several-sigma, not noise) structure. Latest
framing (after the 4-combo/3-zbin mixed-event restructuring): a
positive excess specific to events where the north-side lepton is
positively charged (both in the OS channel and, tellingly, in the
same-charge LS "null test" channel), concentrated at low z-vertex
([-50,30)). Root cause still unknown. Candidate explanations and where
they stand as of 2026-07-14:
  - FTT disk-2 south-half dead zone + field-bending crossover
    (mechanism: charge-dependent bending redistributes which tracks'
    FTT hits fall in the dead region) -- real-data diagnostic
    (checkChargeAsym.C) found a genuine spatial (ECAL cluster Y-
    position) asymmetry between north+ (mean Y~+11cm) and north-
    (mean Y~-10cm) consistent with this, but track-quality metrics
    (nFitPoints, chi2, dcaZ) were NOT asymmetric, and this diagnostic's
    own Np:Nm ratio (~1:1.1) was far less skewed than the full chain's
    (~1:5) -- most of the imbalance is downstream of basic track
    matching. BLC vertex fit success rate vs north-track charge sign,
    checked 2026-07-14 night (fixed a tautology bug first -- selecting
    on trackType==4/BLCVtx pre-conditions on vertex success by
    construction, always giving 100%; re-matched on trackType==0/Global
    instead): all-event baseline 89.5%, north- 89.7% (matches baseline),
    north+ 85.6% (~4pts low, ~1.7 sigma on 30 files). Real and in the
    right direction, but too small alone to explain a ~5x count
    imbalance -- needs the full 202607 dataset to sharpen the
    significance, and likely needs to compound with the
    ETOT/HTOT/Cone/SigmaMax cluster-quality cuts (not applied in this
    quick diagnostic) to reach that scale -- those cuts are now the
    leading untested suspect for the rest of the gap.
  - StFwdResidualMaker quadrant x charge FTT-usage study: REAL RESULT
    now in hand (2026-07-15, 1272/3846 afterburner jobs merged -- see
    proposal_mixed.txt for full numbers, jpsi/ftt_quadrant_charge.html
    for plots/table). North-side positive-charge tracks get an FTT hit
    ~24-26% less often than negative (Global tracks: N-top -26.1%,
    N-bottom -23.6%; South: -4.2%/+7.1%, much smaller). Effect is
    UNIFORM across all 4 FTT disks and both x/y orientations -- this
    argues against the original disk-2-specific dead-zone hypothesis;
    looks like a broader north-side charge-dependent effect instead.
    Same direction as the BLC-vertex-fit-rate finding, now at much
    higher statistics. BLCVtx numbers show the same direction but have
    an unphysical >1.0 fraction in N-bottom (likely double-counting
    across the chained refit stages) -- not yet root-caused, don't
    trust BLCVtx numbers quantitatively yet. 2D hit-position maps built
    but inconclusive (no denominator for "track passed through but
    missed") -- FIXED 2026-07-16: added h2Fst/FttProjXY histograms
    (StFwdResidualMaker.h/.cxx), filled with the track's continuous
    projected (x,y) at that disk instead of the strip-quantized hit
    position, same gating. cons-built, confirmed to remove the banding
    artifact (single-file before/after comparison).

    The full 3846-job afterburner reproduction finished 2026-07-16
    morning, but ALL 3846 files still have the OLD histograms (no
    ProjXYByCharge) -- every job had already started (dlopen'd the old
    .so) before the rebuild, even ones finishing many hours later (the
    biggest files run 4+ hours). Confirmed via a 1-file resubmit test
    (moved that file's picoDst+residual aside, resubmitted just it) that
    a genuinely fresh condor job DOES pick up the new histograms
    correctly -- so the fix itself is verified working, just not
    reflected in the current 3846-file dataset. User's call (2026-07-16):
    don't resubmit the batch for this now -- it's a supplementary
    diagnostic, not the headline result (which is unaffected, since the
    plane-usage/quadrant-charge logic itself never changed). Will
    naturally pick up next time this production is rerun for other
    reasons.

    jpsi/ftt_quadrant_charge.html restructured 2026-07-16 into compact
    tables (Global/BLCVtx columns, height=200 thumbnails, click for full
    size): one for the full-statistics hit-position maps, one for the
    single-file projection-based maps. Once the projection table has
    full-batch statistics, it supersedes the hit-position table entirely
    (same info, no banding artifact) -- drop the hit-position table then
    rather than keeping both.
  - Charge misidentification -- confirmed real and non-trivial via two
    independent methods on pythia.JPsi.vz40 MC: (1) OS/LS leakage in
    the dilepton mass spectrum (a pure e+e- sample should have zero
    like-sign content; solving 2p(1-p)=LS/(LS+OS) gives single-track
    misID p~15-19% for BLCVtx, ~15% for FCSTRK); (2) direct truth-
    matched q/pT_reco-vs-true 2D histogram from picoMatch.C
    (trkQPtRecovsTruBLCVtx/FCSTRK, charge-agnostic, full statistics):
    BLCVtx ~25.0%, FCSTRK ~12.8% -- FCSTRK's ECAL-cluster-constrained
    refit roughly halves the misID rate vs BLCVtx. Not yet shown to be
    the *source* of the north+/north- asymmetry specifically (would
    need the misID rate itself to be charge- or z-asymmetric, not just
    present) -- worth checking next.
  - Real physics / detector-acceptance artifact -- tested directly via
    #10's JPsi+dybg realistic-vertex MC comparison: JPsi MC (pure
    signal) shows NO north-charge asymmetry at all (all 12
    zbin x combo panels look alike), ruling out a simple universal
    charge-bending artifact that would act on any north-side track
    regardless of context. dybg MC (background-rich) shows a partial,
    not-quite-matching echo: a real excess in z=[-50,30) NpSm only, but
    at low mass (~0-2 GeV) rather than the ~2 GeV region seen in real
    data, and not mirrored in the same-charge NpSp channel the way real
    data's effect appears in both OS and LS north+ channels. Result:
    background/multiplicity-density-correlated, not purely data-only,
    but not a clean reproduction either -- inconclusive.
  - Miscalibration (pi0 mass check): both JPsi and dybg MC pi0 mass
    plots look fine/consistent with the established peak -- no
    indication of an energy-scale problem.
Also found and fixed along the way: dybg's realistic-vertex MC filters
on generator-level ET of ANY stable particle (including hadrons, which
only deposit partial MIP energy in ECAL), but the analysis-level
picoDilepton.C cluster ET cut (mETCut) was 0.4 GeV -- well below the
real ~1-1.1 GeV trigger threshold and the JPsi filter's effective
electron-ET cut. Raising it (0.4->1.0, then dialed back to 0.8 GeV)
cut dybg's surviving OS/LS candidate yield by ~85% (4094->600 total),
confirming most of that population was sub-trigger-threshold hadronic-
MIP contamination that real data would never have seen. dybg needs
substantially more statistics at the corrected cut before the z0/NpSm
excess (or anything else in the 12-panel breakdown) can be trusted --
another MC batch queued for 2026-07-14 evening.

8. FCS track-cluster matching cut needs a real tuning pass -- paused for Daniel discussion
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
picoDilepton.C's track-FCS matching (mDRCut=20cm/mRdphiCut=8cm) is
loose and hand-tuned. Investigated switching to the reconstruction-level
StFcsTrackMatchMaker match (t->ecalMatchIndex()) instead, but paused
before implementing pending a bigger-picture discussion with Daniel.
Key findings:
  - doFCSConstrainedFitting() (FwdTracker.h ~L1121) refits each BLCVtx
    track with its matched ECAL cluster as a real Kalman-fit hit (1.2cm
    position sigma) to produce FCSTRK, but the matched cluster's
    identity is NOT persisted anywhere retrievable -- only its effect
    on the fitted trajectory survives.
  - StFcsTrackMatchMaker (runs later in the chain) independently
    re-matches every track type's post-fit ecalProjection() to the
    nearest cluster within a fixed 6cm window. For FCSTRK this should be
    near-tautological (the fit was anchored to that cluster), but isn't
    guaranteed in busy events, and it's the ONLY source of queryable
    cluster association at all for the other 5 track types -- not
    obsolete, still essential.
  - StFcsTrackMatchMaker's own cuts (6.0cm ecal/10.0cm hcal, energy
    thresholds) are themselves hand-tuned, not a solved reference.
    Hcal matching hasn't been studied at all yet.
  - The right window is tracking/alignment-dependent and will need
    periodic retuning as those improve -- MC can't validate it well.
Recommended approach (mine, pending Daniel's input): use picoMatch.C
(script-level, no recompile/reprocess, fast iteration) to run a loose
window, tag real-J/psi-associated track-cluster pairs via the dilepton
mass peak, derive a properly-tuned window from that residual
distribution (e.g. 3-5 sigma), then promote the result into
StFcsTrackMatchMaker's compiled defaults. Extending StFwdResidualMaker
for FCS residuals is a reasonable longer-term consistency step but not
the first move, given this needs to be low-friction to actually get
redone each time alignment changes.

10. MC sample of J/psi & DYBG with realistic vertex distribution
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
runPythia.C's vz argument changed from vertex CENTER to vertex SIGMA
(mean fixed at 0,0,0; sigmaX=sigmaY=0.1cm, sigmaZ=vz) -- vz40 means a
realistic sigma_z=40cm spread. Both samples produced, pico-analyzed
(BLCVtx and FCSTRK track types), and compared against real data
202607 through the identical 4-combo/3-zbin mixed-event chain -- see
#6 for the physics results (inconclusive on the north+/north-
mechanism, but ruled out a simple universal charge-bending artifact
and surfaced the mETCut/hadronic-MIP-contamination bug in dybg).
Current sizes: pythia.JPsi.vz40 at 200/200 runs; pythia.dybg.vz40 at
20000/20000 runs (10 missing/evicted runs caught via the new
check_missing.pl and resubmitted) but only ~600 OS+LS candidates
survive post-mETCut=0.8 -- more dybg statistics queued for 2026-07-14
evening (target TBD, was ~1:7 candidates/run survival at the old cut).
Also cleaned up MC production log volume along the way (dybg run1 log
was 574 MB / 7.8M lines): genfit's errorOut wasn't redirected (every
failed/looper RKTrackRep propagation dumped a multi-line exception to
stderr, unconditionally -- the dominant contributor), two unconditional
debug leftovers in FitterUtils.h/StFwdTrackMaker.cxx, an unguarded
LOG_INFO dump in ObjExporter.h, and sim.C had
StFcsFastSimulatorMaker's setDebug(1) hardcoded on (~500k lines/run of
per-hit ECAL/HCAL/Pres prints). All fixed; not yet verified against an
actual production run (tonight's dybg batch will be the first check).
