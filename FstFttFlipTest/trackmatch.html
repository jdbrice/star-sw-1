What the numbers say

  • The mismatch is radial, not azimuthal. fcsR*dPhi is 7–7.7 cm while dR is 10.5–14.4, a factor up to two. dX and dY are mixtures of the two, so the radial term is what inflates them. A radial displacement is what a wrong z produces, since tracks come from near the origin — the assumed shower-max depth (710.16 + 15 cm) is the first thing to test.
  • Zero field matches five times more cleanly. S/B is 0.39–0.51 for ZF 22 against 0.078–0.095 for the forward stream. In the forward stream roughly 90% of the raw histogram is combinatorial, so any number taken from it without background subtraction is meaningless.
  • HCAL matching has essentially no signal in either dataset: S/B 0.025–0.079, and after subtraction the content inside |d|<30 cm cancels — 10 to 12 negative bins with a negative sum as large as the positive one. The HCAL widths are therefore not quoted; the plots are shown for completeness only.
  • FTT time calibration is not the limit. Per-run fttDataWindow anchors now exist for 10 of the 11 forward-stream runs, up from 2, with 86–94% of the 384 VMM entries usable. Re-running the whole arm with them changes every panel by 2% or less — dx 0.99, dy 1.00, fcsR*dPhi 0.98–1.01, dR 0.99 — so the FTT timing is not what limits the FCS match. The one run left uncalibrated, 23081053, has a single MuDst that stopped at 14 events and keeps the global DB window on purpose.
