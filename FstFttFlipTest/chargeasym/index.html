FST/FTT charge asymmetry vs track type

Run22 forward stream, full ~1 day (44 runs, 3701 files, pico_fwdstream_full5_20261006), afterburner ALIGN=0 MAGFIELD=1 GEOFIX=1,0,0 FTTTIMECUT=2 FTTWINDOW=-1,3, fGeom.dev2022sm.misalign1.sdt20211112.000003. Supersedes the July page (Global and BLCVtx only, jpsi/ftt_quadrant_charge_fixed_2.html): all five track types, full statistics, one campaign.

A = (N+ − N−) / (N+ + N−) per detector quadrant, from PlaneUsageQuadCharge, i.e. tracks of each charge that used a given plane. Numbers below are the FST1 bin, which is on essentially every track. North–south swing = ⟨A⟩S − ⟨A⟩N.

Summary plots

A per quadrant vs track type, and the north–south swing. A per detector plane, one pad per quadrant, one curve per type.

Points

  • The asymmetry is already present for Global tracks, which carry no vertex constraint. It is not created by the vertex.
  • The vertex constraint multiplies the north–south swing by about four, 0.096 → 0.33–0.40. All four constrained types land in the same band.
  • Nbot is the most negative in every type, and worst for FCSConstrained (−0.353, i.e. 26.2M q+ against 54.7M q−). It is the cleanest handle on the effect.
  • FCS-constrained tracking does not fix it (+0.331, in the same band as the others), and is the most top–bottom and least north–south asymmetric of the constrained set.
  • In the per-plane view, Global is the outlier in all four quadrants and the four constrained types sit nearly on top of each other.

FTT x- vs y-measuring planes

Mean A over the four FTT x-planes minus the mean over the four y-planes. Only Global's top quadrants fall outside ±0.065, with opposite sign north and south; every constrained type is flat by comparison. Since the constrained types use the same hits, this points at the unconstrained fit's angular freedom rather than a plane-level geometry error — not yet tested.
