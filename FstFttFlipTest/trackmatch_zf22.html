    The matched fraction is small by construction: forward tracks are mostly charged hadrons, which ECAL sees only as a MIP for about half of them and as partial energy loss for the rest, while ECAL itself is dominated by photons from pi0 that leave no track. fcsR*dPhi is much narrower than dR, so the mismatch is radial rather than azimuthal — which is what a wrong z, i.e. the assumed shower-max depth, would produce. HCAL is not quoted: its S/B is 0.03–0.08 and after subtraction the content inside |d|<30 cm cancels, so those widths are artefacts.
