WHY THIS IS NOT "TURN THE TIME CUT OFF"
---------------------------------------

The FTT needs a time cut. Out-of-time pile-up is real, the wide window is not
a physics choice, and AcceptAll is a diagnostic arm, never a configuration to
ship. The result above says the present windows are applied WRONG, and the job
is to find out how. Do not read the table as an argument for mode 1 or 2.


THE ARGUMENT THAT IT IS MIS-CENTRED
-----------------------------------

The windows drop 87-93% of the points (2100 -> 260 against the old window).
That much is intended; one bunch crossing instead of a wide window.

But a tight window that keeps signal and drops out-of-time background leaves
the good hits where they were, so chi2 should improve, or at worst stay flat.
It TRIPLED. Keeping 12% of the points and tripling chi2 means the surviving
12% is not the physics subset.

This also explains the anomaly recorded in planar_integration_20261109.txt and
never understood: added hits per track went UP, 1.90 against 1.650, when the
window got tighter. A correct tighter window cannot do that.

The per-VMM time anchors are the obvious place for a sign or a unit error, and
they are enabled for this run:

  StFttDbMaker::loadDataWindows - per-VMM time anchors ENABLED: entry stamped
  for run 23081001

Candidates, in order: anchor sign; units (dbcid ticks vs ns); per-VMM indexing
-- the open VMM 140-143 question lives in exactly this area.


THE SURVIVING ALTERNATIVE
-------------------------

The windows are right and the TRACKER substitutes: a correct tight window
removes the real hit, and the tracker then takes a noise hit on that plane
rather than leaving it empty. Flat nSeed with tripled chi2 is what that looks
like too. If so the bug is in hit association, not in the calibration, and the
fix is a different file.


THE ONE MEASUREMENT THAT SEPARATES THEM
---------------------------------------

Per-chamber, better per-VMM, counts of the surviving points in mode 0.

  mis-centred anchors  ->  uneven survivor map, some VMMs near zero while
                           others look normal
  correct selection    ->  the 260 spread evenly over the 16 chambers

Either from a rerun with the cluster-maker diagnostics on, or by reading the
blind histograms already in fcstrk.root.


SCOPE -- THIS IS NOT ONLY OUR PROBLEM
-------------------------------------

Production FTT reco uses fttTimeCutMode=0. Every run reconstructed since the
windows were pushed on 2026-10-16 carries whatever this is. Check before
telling anyone, but do not sit on it either.


WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT EXPLAIN
---------------------------------

172 -> 71 is 58% of the excess over the 2026-10-01 figure of 16.7.

At 71 against 16.7 a factor 4.2 REMAINS, and the 10-01 campaign predates the
windows, so that residual is a different change in the same five weeks. The
bisect is still owed: 60-event probe, script/planarAB.C, 4 minutes an arm,
between the 10-01 campaign commit and HEAD.

Candidates already dead for the residual, each with its reason:
  * measurement sigma -- would have to shrink below the 0.924 mm single-strip
    floor in StFttClusterMaker, which clamps from below.
  * planar vs space-point measurement model -- chi2-neutral, measured.
  * geometry displacement at the cm scale -- bounded at 10%: the Nov 9 A/B
    moves every FTT hit by 17.55 mm and costs 163 -> 148.
  * pentOnStation row Id=5's missing 0.86 cm of dy -- real, worth 4-5 of ~159.

Caveat on every number here: macro defaults, so applyFstGapFix=false where
production uses GEOFIX 1,0,0. Identical in all three arms, so the ratios hold;
the absolute values are not production-configured.
